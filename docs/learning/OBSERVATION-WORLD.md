## Approved narrative and learning limit
World name: **غابة الملاحظة**, theme “أصداء الأدلة”. The player is an explorer carrying the **عدسة الدليل / Evidence Lens** and reclaiming paths distorted by وحش الافتراضات. Gameplay must use environmental decisions, not nine modal quizzes. World segments approved by Game Director: (A) بوابة الشهادات L01–03, (B) مستنقع الظنون L04–06, (C) ورشة التأويل L07–09, (D) عرين الافتراضات L10 Boss.

**Primary assessed skill = OBSERVATION** (direct evidence, correct boundary, justified uncertainty). Potential Insight and Concept in L08–09 and Boss are **guided previews** of later worlds, not trusted INS/CON mastery. Creation can be formatively coached with R-PREV rubric; free-text answer is not auto-graded. Each level's choice visibly changes path, available evidence, NPC behavior or arena; at least 7/9 need traversable/evidence state change per Director acceptance.

## Per-encounter inputs and fairness requirements
**L01 / Inspect (recognition):** E1 “رجعت الكتاب من غير ما أخلّصه”; E2 “قريت أول فصل وبعدين سيبته”. The Lens points to two documented actions, not “القراء فقدوا شغفهم”. Player must inspect two clues before choosing. This is locating evidence, not guessing an insight.

**L02 / Connect (recognition with source matching):** E1 NPC “بعد شراء الزرعة رجعت أسأل عن الري”; E2 care card found at nursery counter. Connect action to witness/physical trace. “الأهالي خايفين يقتلوا الزرع” is possible but unproven. Delta from L01: track **which source** makes claim credible; avoid an identical sorting riddle.

**L03 / Arrange (diagnosis of change):** E1 cinema booking for 23:00 changed to 16:00; E2 another late booking changed to a morning screening; E3 an unrelated early booking not changed. Arrange before/after tickets and state “some late bookings shifted earlier” without saying *why*. Delta: directional comparison and out-of-pattern record, not simple classification.

**L04 / Compare (unsupported psychological leap):** E1 tried same jacket in two sizes; E2 tried same trousers in three sizes; the guardian projects “هم فاقدين الثقة في نفسهم”. Select behavior and explicitly identify absence of psychological evidence. Delta: diagnose seductive motive, not just chronology.

**L05 / Deflect (three levels of certainty):** E1 multiple listeners skip tracks before the end. Three mirrors: “ناس بتعمل Skip بدري” (Observed), “يمكن بيدوروا على حاجة مناسبة للمود” (Possible), “اللي بيعمل Skip مبيحبش الموسيقى” (Unsupported). All three categories must be correctly labelled; a guessed motive may be possible, not inherently wrong. Delta: uncertainty calibration.

**L06 / Route (context split):** E1 weekday customers take coffee away en route; E2 same setting on days off has some customers sitting. Choose contextually accurate routes. Wrong: universal “الشغل بيخلّي كل الناس مستعجلة”; safe: “في الأمثلة دي بعض الناس بتغيّر طريقة شرب القهوة حسب اليوم”. Delta: two contexts and limited scope, not just fact vs speculation.

**L07 / Interview (reconstruction of missing evidence):** Shop visitors read board-game rules; some leave without purchase. Candidate explanations: instructions are confusing; visitors merely browsing. Player chooses a neutral question (“احكيلي آخر مرة قريت قواعد لعبة في المحل: إيه اللي حصل بعد كده؟”) and identifies evidence needed to distinguish explanations. Wrong: “واضح إن القواعد صعبة، صح؟”. Delta: construct a useful follow-up, not assert a motive.

**L08 / Test (reasoning preview):** Gallery visitors photograph works, some continue, some return later. Possible Insight A: photos help recall particular works; B: sharing photos with friends may be part of visit; neither proven. Player chooses test source (non-leading visitor question vs consented photo sharing context) and explains what each could establish. Either route can be valid with qualifiers and evidence fit. **Do NOT award INS mastery** merely because hypothesis sounds insightful. Delta: evaluate two potentially defensible paths, not find one predetermined answer.

**L09 / Forge (Concept preview, Observation mastery still central):** Fictional coworking E1 monthly subscriber used space on only a few days; E2 another renews but visits four days per month. Brand proof card **in this exercise only**: fictional service offers a confirmed per-day access pass. Player forges **Observation** (usage below paid period for a few sampled people) → **possible hypothesis** (fixed monthly plans may not fit intermittent needs) → **proven fictional brand action** (per-day pass) → **tangible Concept seed**. Two valid forms: (A) dynamic calendar showing only actual booked desk days as paid tiles, (B) a “desk appears only on days you choose” visual, each with explicit product proof/feasibility check. Rejected: “اشتغل في أي وقت ومن أي مكان مجانًا” (fabricated benefit) or “خلفية بنفسجي Premium” (style, not concept). Score Preview concept quality only by human/rubric; no semantic auto-grader. Delta: first *guided* translation of evidence into useful brand action, not certified Creative Concept mastery.

## Three-phase Boss — وحش الافتراضات (the Director's crafts fair)
**Environment:** fictional craft fair. E1 visitors photograph pieces; E2 some record size/item numbers; E3 some continue to other booths; E4 a subset returns to same booth later. All examples are limited observations, no universal “price fear”. New sector relative to L01–09, so Boss is a legitimate **transfer task**.

1. **B01 THE FOG — Observe / retrieval.** Monster floats statements above craft props. Player equips at least two evidence-supported actions and refuses price-based mind reading. R-OBS-TRACE 40, CLASS 40, SCOPE 20. Two correct paths may exist; correct clue cracks actual fog. Unassisted retrieval, not a verbatim L01 repeat.
2. **B02 THE MIRROR — Potential Insight + falsifier.** Illusions: maybe visitors are comparing options, or maybe they want a memento, vs “كلهم مش قادرين يدفعوا”. Choose either bounded plausible motive AND a specific test (e.g. ask what led someone to photograph/return; check consented comparison behavior). ALT 45, SCOPE 30, TRACE 25. **Either defensible route** can break mirror with test; not just select “the designer's insight”. This is reasoning preview, not validated psychology.
3. **B03 THE FORGE — Concept transfer and defense.** Fictional organizer-provided proof card: fair can provide a verified booth map and item-number reference tool; this is an **exercise fact, not a promise about real events**. Player links observed notes/revisits to one conditional audience hypothesis and tangible organizer action. Valid route A: “Bookmark the Craft” — save item code and map to revisit a booth; route B: “Compare Craft Trail” — a fair-provided trail card listing photographed item codes for later comparison. Distinct tradeoffs (revisit vs comparison), neither requires claiming all visitors behave that way. Unsupported: generic “أفضل معرض للحرف” tagline or an invented price guarantee. R-OBS-TRACE 35, SCOPE 25, R-PREV-BRAND 20, R-PREV-CONCEPT 20, plus critical R-OBS-TRANSFER check. Both concept paths may pass **human review**, not deterministic free-text classification. A finite choice can check obvious proof/claim constraints, but not “creativity”. Player action rebuilds exit bridge, no fake HP auto-judge.

## Pedagogical and UX acceptance
- Exactly nine visibly different encounter verbs: Inspect, Connect, Arrange, Compare, Deflect, Route, Interview, Test, Forge.
- At least four independent cognitive operations in first nine and a meaningful never-before-seen transfer in Boss B03; easy sector swaps do not count.
- At least 7 of 9 encounters change a meaningful path/evidence/NPC state (not only XP/HP), with keyboard/tap alternatives and no timed reading pressure.
- Normal gameplay must never route all nine back to legacy check() fields. Existing IDs and localStorage retained until HQ migration.
- Each structured item gets criterion-specific feedback; each free-form concept remains provisional without human assessment. Potential Insight/Concept previews cannot award INS/CON mastery.
- At least 2 defensible, 2 partially supported and 2 unsupported answers per authored item must be reviewed by QA; any disputed key rewritten before release.
- Provide post-Boss unseen mini-brief in playtesting to check transfer independently; it need not become a tenth pre-Boss encounter.
