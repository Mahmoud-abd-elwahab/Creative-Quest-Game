## Desired player fantasy and agency learning
An explorable “Evidence Forest” where the player rescues corrupted notes from an Assumption Guardian. Each physical interaction reveals or transforms part of the reasoning; decoration is not the question. Every challenge introduces one different cognitive verb, and the Boss combines them without assuming the player has already mastered advertising strategy.

Teaching copy: natural Egyptian Arabic, terms such as Observation, Evidence, Claim, Insight and Hypothesis kept in English. Illustrate the difference between “اللي شفناه” and “اللي بنفترضه” with an evidence-to-claim diagram. Do not teach “every plausible inference is false”; teach that an inference may be worth investigating while remaining unverified.

### E01 — What counts as a record?
Inputs: E1 “رجعت الرواية من غير ما أخلصها”; E2 “اتفرجت على أول فصل وبعدين رجعت الكتاب”. Distinguish observed behavior from “القراء زهقانين من الكتب” and “الكتاب سيئ”. Correct action = select actual return/reading behavior, cite E1/E2; correct alternative phrasing accepted. Unsupported reasonings are psychologically plausible but not recorded. **Delta:** first clear distinction; later investigate motives rather than suppress them.

### E02 — Scope is part of the evidence
Inputs: sample Day A: 8 of 20 purchases before 11:00; Day B: 5 of 20. Player chooses a sentence faithful to counts, avoiding “morning demand collapsed in the entire city” or “weather caused it”. **Delta from E01:** numerical precision and denominator. Suggested feedback: “الرقمين دول عن يومين في كشك واحد، مش كل السوق”. No actual market fact asserted.

### E03 — More than one source
Inputs: E1 one quoted customer asked to replay a radio episode; E2 interaction log shows repeated play by some accounts; E3 colleague says “الناس زهقت من الصوت”. Require player to link E1/E2 to a bounded repeat behavior and isolate E3 as an unverified interpretation. **Delta from E02:** disparate source types and reliability, not just counting.

### E04 — Missing evidence is an answer
Inputs: 100 landing-page opens, 27 click-to-book actions, no completed-booking count. Client asks “ليه 73 شخص رافضين يسجلوا؟”. Correct action: mark conversion and motive unknown; request completion-event measurement and audience context. **Delta from E03:** prevent inference when the right data does not exist. “27 clicks” is supportable; “73 refusals” is not.

### E05 — Repair, don't merely condemn
Inputs: three fictional product return notes about difficulty checking equipment sizes; client copy says “كل الناس بترجع المنتج لأنهم بيكرهوا المقاسات”. Player moves three parts of claim: supported action, unsupported cause, bounded replacement. **Delta from E04:** produce an actionable corrected sentence; do not use a synonym of “hate”. In later brand work, this protects product promises.

### E06 — Seek the exception and segment
Inputs: weekday: 4 of 12 visitors checked tool availability online; weekend: 9 of 12 did; one manual counter-record contradicts broad “everyone checks online” claim. Player partitions records then chooses segment-specific statement and names limitation. **Delta from E05:** counterexample integration across contexts. Do not force a causal day-of-week story.

### E07 — Independent synthesis
Inputs: fictional parcel service, E1 6/10 visits after 18:00 over observed day A; E2 2/10 visits before 10:00 over observed day B; E3 one staff anecdote requests later hours. Player writes concise Observation, labels counts and unknown motives, and cites E1/E2. Open text evaluated against R-OBS rubric by reviewer or self-check with unverified badge; never use token matching as mastery. **Delta from E06:** move from selecting to constructing an evidence-led statement.

### E08 — Test alternatives, not fortune-telling
Inputs: 14 of 25 workshop signups now choose a later time; early slots still exist; client suggests “محدش عايز يصحى بدري”. Possible explanations: commuting schedule, timetable visibility, price; none verified. Player chooses one neutral, non-leading research question and which log to inspect; must describe what each test could distinguish. **Delta from E07:** actively plan evidence collection and rival explanations.

### E09 — Unfamiliar advertising transfer
Inputs: fictional pop-up cultural event; 140 poster QR scans, 18 completed registrations, one quote “جربت أشوف المواعيد”; brief says “كل الشباب عايزين الفعالية بس خايفين من الزحمة” and “اعمل KV يقول إن الفعالية مناسبة للجميع”. Player tags recorded fact, possible explanation, unsupported promise; rewrites one safe audience observation and proposes one additional check **before** any Creative Concept. **Delta from E08:** must push back on an attractive client claim in a new mixed-media marketing task; consequence is better brief discipline. Do not reward a pretty KV here.

## Boss — “حارس الافتراضات” (3 genuinely different cognition stages)
Boss must consume **the same R-OBS rubric IDs and version**; owner: Boss Designer for game mechanics. Accessible untimed controls. Wrong answers reveal their reasoning flaw and allow retry, not HP punishment for experimenting. Phase state is a presentation/event concern awaiting HQ contract.

**B01 — The Gate of Records / retrieval and classification.** New fictional museum outreach records: 12/30 scanned a flyer QR; 4/30 completed a signup; one docent heard a visitor ask about opening hours. Player sorts evidence/proposition/speculation and anchors a claim to E-IDs. Target CLASS 40 / TRACE 40 / SCOPE 20. **Different from E09:** focused no-hint retrieval on unseen input, not another client rewrite. Correct keys must be explicit and allow alternative warranted descriptions.

**B02 — The Mirror of Causation / reconstruct faulty logic.** Boss claims “الـ26 شخص اللي ما سجلوش بيكرهوا المتحف، وQR مش شغال”. Player identifies that the 26-count is wrong in logical scope (30 sampled; 4 registered, but no proof scan caused registration), removes the invented motive and repairs it: “4/30 أكملوا التسجيل حسب السجل، وسبب عدم تسجيل الباقي مش معروف”. Target REPAIR 50 / SCOPE 30 / TRACE 20. Must not confuse QR scans with unique people or established signup path. **Different from B01:** repair and qualify rather than classify.

**B03 — The Stranger's Brief / transfer.** Different sector: fictional service for repairing home appliances; a customer-support note cites 3 delayed pickup enquiries, while a click report shows 40 service-page visits without booking data. Player provides (a) one safe observation (b) one alternative explanation as **hypothesis**, (c) one new measurement/interview question and (d) the unsupported claim to reject (“كل الناس خايفة من الأسعار”). Target TRANSFER 40 / ALT 25 / TRACE 20 / SCOPE 15. Mark open response provisional until a human reviewer or authenticated evaluator exists. **Different from B02:** independent critique in entirely unfamiliar sector, not copying a model answer.
