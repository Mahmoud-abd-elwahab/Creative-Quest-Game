## Cognitive operation diversity
First nine contain nine separately stated **player verbs**, but “different input gesture” alone is insufficient. Evidence requirements differ: (1) observation trace, (2) provenance mapping, (3) chronological reconciliation, (4) unsupported motive diagnosis, (5) uncertainty classification, (6) context-dependent comparison, (7) neutral research planning, (8) two-hypothesis test design, (9) constrained creative synthesis. This exceeds the requirement of four distinct cognitive operations *on paper*. Unverified until authored tasks and player behavior are inspected.

## Counterexample fairness gate
- If a player explains a fact accurately in Egyptian Arabic but does not use sample phrase, reward under human rubric or deterministic finite evidence selection; reject the old substring grader.
- If a tentative insight is plausible but unverifiable, accept **as a hypothesis** with validation plan; do not mark “fact”.
- If a creative route is different from example but preserves proven service truth, relevant tension and tangible execution, it may pass human review. “Wrong by nonmatching tag line” is unacceptable.
- If audience motivation, customer category or product capabilities are invented, fail trace/scope/brand criterion even when execution is visually appealing.
- If source records are ambiguous or competing readings plausible, writer must modify E-IDs/correct options or allow multiple correct routes; never “designer intent” as hidden answer.
- If QA finds 2 adjacent operations are equivalent after reading actual script, send both to Writer with a rewrite request before merge, not simply change background or question kind.

## Source vs research certainty
Private Creative Library patterns are read-only internal training inspiration, not validated current consumer evidence. Fictional NPC dialogue is not a real interview. Brand facts in L09 and Boss are **scenario constraints** that cannot be repurposed into public real-world claims. No universal behavioral psychology without supporting data.
